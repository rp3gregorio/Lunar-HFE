# References

PDF copies of key papers cited in the manuscript. They ground physics
claims against the actual source material rather than relying on memory.

## How to use

Place PDFs here with filenames matching their citation keys:

```
references/
  hayne2017.pdf          — Hayne et al. 2017, JGR Planets
  langseth1976.pdf       — Langseth et al. 1976, JGR
  martinez2021.pdf       — Martínez & Siegler 2021, JGR Planets
  nagihara2018.pdf       — Nagihara et al. 2018, JGR Planets
  hemingway1973.pdf      — Hemingway et al. 1973
  vasavada2012.pdf       — Vasavada et al. 2012, JGR Planets
  grott2010.pdf          — Grott et al. 2010
  feng2020.pdf           — Feng et al. 2020
```

## Note

These PDFs are for local reference only and should NOT be committed to
the public repository (they are copyrighted). Add `references/*.pdf` to
`.gitignore`.
