\shead{1\enspace What was built}
Two parallel poster artifacts exist by design, serving different needs:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{LaTeX poster} (\texttt{poster/aogs\_poster.tex} $\to$
    \texttt{.pdf}) --- print-ready, exact A0 portrait (841$\times$1189 mm),
    AOGS-compliant masthead (presenter underlined + bold, abstract ID,
    session window, contact). Layout: 2 blocks per column
    (Physical model / Sites $\;\vert\;$ Validation / Sensitivity) plus a
    full-width ``Robustness \& conclusions'' band holding the pipeline
    flowchart, the bootstrap figure, and three conclusion bullets.
  \item \textbf{Canva PPTX line} (\texttt{aogs\_poster\_canva\_*.pptx}) ---
    element-editable version for iteration in Canva; the presenter's
    working copy. Final: \textbf{v3}.
\end{itemize}
Both draw every quoted number from \texttt{poster\_numbers.tex}, which is
\emph{generated} by \texttt{make\_poster\_figures.py} from the results
archives --- no number on either poster is hand-typed.

\shead{2\enspace Version history (what happened, in order)}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item[LaTeX v1 (07-10/11).] First full poster; plain academic grid, all
    seven figures, governing-equation box. Compiled to exact A0 with the
    references strip on-page --- the recurring constraint that shaped every
    later edit (tikzposter silently clips overflow off the page bottom).
  \item[Content audit (07-12).] A physics/code audit before polishing found
    and fixed three real issues: the quoted ``$\sim$1 K shadow cooling''
    was wrong (audited: \shadowcoolAfif\ K at A15 / \shadowcoolAsev\ K at
    A17 --- so terrain warming \emph{wins} at A17); a ``flux closure
    $\le$0.8\%'' claim failed for 180/650 runs (a metric artifact at the
    sub-anchor conductivity step; reworded to ``winners $\le\closurewin\%$,
    $n$96 drift $\le\driftmax$ mK''); and the cross-site numbers had no
    generating script (\texttt{s5\_crossite.py} written; reproduces all
    four numbers to $10^{-4}$).
  \item[LaTeX v2 rebalance (07-12).] The packed first layout read as
    ``small figures + blank gaps.'' Rebalanced per conference-poster
    practice: header tightened, redundant RMSE table dropped, figures
    enlarged 25--45\%, columns equalized; then the model schematic and the
    pipeline flowchart were integrated (the flowchart into the conclusions
    band). All ``letter'' wording removed from figures at the presenter's
    request.
  \item[Flowchart upgrade (07-12).] The pipeline flowchart was rebuilt to
    carry the governing equations in every step box, plus the exact
    $K_d$ logic: $K_d^{\star}$ is \emph{site-specific}
    ($K_d^{\star}(\mathrm{A15}){=}\kdstarAfif$,
    $K_d^{\star}(\mathrm{A17}){=}\kdstarAsev$ mW\,m$^{-1}$K$^{-1}$); the
    \textbf{cp branch} holds it fixed, the \textbf{coupled branch} sets
    $K_d=K_c(\rho_3)$ anchored at $K_c(\rho_d{=}1800)=K_d^{\star}$ (so the
    A17 winner's effective deep conductivity is 7.99, not 7.08). The DEM
    box now states the resolution: 16 ppd $\approx$ 1.9 km/px.
  \item[Bootstrap figure fix (07-12).] The bootstrap panel's x-axis
    originally ran to the homogeneous baseline (3.76 K at A17), crushing
    the CIs into the left 13\% of the axis. Rebuilt: axes zoom to the
    points; A17's off-scale baseline is marked with an arrow.
  \item[PPTX corruption incident (07-12).] The presenter's hand-arranged
    Canva export (\texttt{aogs\_poster\_canva.pptx}, kept on the
    iCloud-synced Desktop) arrived corrupt: two embedded images were
    random bytes and one relationship pointed at \texttt{Target="NULL"}.
    Repaired by CRC-bypass extraction, identifying each broken picture via
    its \texttt{descr} attribute (Canva preserves source filenames),
    re-rendering replacements at 300 dpi from the repo PDFs, and repairing
    the relationship table $\to$ \textbf{v2}. Two stretched figures were
    also restored to true aspect (the validation profiles had been 20\%
    distorted). Captions Fig 1--6 were added/improved in the presenter's
    caption style, including the DEM size in the methodology caption.
    \emph{Lesson recorded: keep the canonical copy in the repo, not on the
    iCloud Desktop.}
  \item[Simulated peer review (07-12).] A five-reviewer panel (editor,
    methodology, domain, cross-disciplinary, devil's advocate) reviewed the
    presenter's latest Canva export. Verdict: \emph{major revision,
    numerically pristine} --- the methodology reviewer traced $\sim$60
    extracted values (including all 18 heatmap cells rasterized inside the
    figures) to the certified archives with a 100\% pass. The substantive
    findings are in \S3.
  \item[v3 (07-12/13).] All review-driven text fixes applied directly to
    the presenter's latest export (16 edits, \S4) $\to$
    \texttt{aogs\_poster\_canva\_v3.pptx}.
  \item[Presenter finalization (07-13).] The presenter edited v3 further in
    PowerPoint (final save 00:00) and exported the print file
    \texttt{Gregorio\_AOGS.pdf} (00:02) --- authored at A4 geometry that
    scales exactly $4\times$ to A0. The repo copy of v3 is therefore the
    latest; the earlier Desktop copy predates these edits.
  \item[Repo reorganization (07-13/14).] The whole AOGS tree was promoted
    to a top-level \texttt{aogs/} folder at the repository root as real
    files (no longer symlinks): \texttt{poster/}, \texttt{briefing/},
    \texttt{talk\_figures/}, \texttt{code/} (the s1--s8 pipeline, figure
    generators, and the study notebook), \texttt{results/} (the 8 JSON
    archives), and \texttt{data/} (LOLA + SLDEM2015). The moved scripts'
    import paths were updated accordingly.
\end{description}

\shead{3\enspace What the review found (the substance)}
\textbf{Genuine content discrepancies (3):} the final Results bullet was
truncated mid-sentence, amputating the winner-stability statistic
(\stabcKAfif\% for the exact A15 triple); ``$\le$0.7 K'' for the
heat-capacity lever was falsified at the second decimal (actual spread
0.728 K); and ``+1.3/+0.7 K terrain re-radiation'' quoted the
re-radiation-\emph{plus-reflected-sunlight} totals under the wrong label.

\textbf{Regressions from the certified backbone:} the Canva export had
silently dropped references [4] (Saito) and [5] (Smith --- leaving the LOLA
DEM uncited), the caveats line (terrain terms are a diagnostic upper bound,
not folded into the 650 fits), the $K_d$--density degeneracy statement, and
Kasai's separate affiliation.

\textbf{Framing challenges (devil's advocate):} the headline
$\sim$10$\times$ gain is measured against a homogeneous baseline nobody
uses --- against the field-standard Hayne continuous model the honest gains
are $\sim$2.3$\times$ (A15) and $\sim$1.5$\times$ (A17), and the
transplanted A15 structure loses to Hayne at A17; the coupled-branch anchor
invites a circularity question (defused by the cp branch acting as a
control: same anchor, no conductivity channel, far worse fit); and ``both
sites favour $z_2=30$ cm'' oversold A17, whose map admits a tied deeper
solution.

\shead{4\enspace The 16 fixes applied in v3}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=1pt]
  \item Truncated bullet completed (``\ldots the exact triple survives only
        13\% of them.'')
  \item References [4] Saito and [5] Smith restored.
  \item LOLA DEM cited at first use (Smith et al.\ [5]).
  \item Degeneracy clause added to the conclusions (broken by the
        opposite-signed site responses; joint retrieval = next step).
  \item Hayne-relative gains added ($\sim$2.3$\times$/$\sim$1.5$\times$).
  \item Transfer bullet rewritten with its numbers (0.90$\to$1.73 K;
        0.36$\to$1.64 K) claiming graceful degradation, not proof.
  \item $z_2$ claim scoped (``Apollo 17 admits a tied deeper solution'').
  \item Warming channel relabeled (re-radiation \emph{and reflected
        sunlight}; diagnostic upper bound, $T_{\rm terr}\approx T_s$).
  \item ``$\le$0.7 K'' $\to$ ``$<$0.8 K'' (both occurrences).
  \item Intro's Hayne characterization corrected (smooth exponential with
        globally fixed endpoints --- not ``globally uniform'').
  \item Surface-density comparison fixed (vs Hayne's surface \emph{value},
        not an endpoint-vs-slab comparison).
  \item Generalization bullet split honestly (forward pipeline extends;
        retrieval framework awaits future in-situ payloads).
  \item Abstract ID brackets removed (PS18-A007).
  \item Kasai corrected to affiliation 2, with both affiliations in the band.
  \item RMSE table labeled ``Table 1'' (matching the in-text reference).
  \item Grott et al.\ core-density citation numbered [6] and added to the
        reference list.
\end{enumerate}
All 16 were verified by reading the saved file back; the PPTX zip is valid;
an overflow estimate shows headroom in every edited text box (the
references strip wraps to a second line, as before).
