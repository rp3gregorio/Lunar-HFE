# Lunar HFE study pack

This folder collects the local PDF reading pack for the Apollo/Lunar-HFE work.
Read the files in the order below if you want the fastest path from physical
intuition to manuscript defense.

## Read first

1. `carrier1991_lunar_sourcebook_ch09_physical_properties.pdf`
   - Physical properties of lunar regolith: density, porosity, grain-size
     distributions, and why the regolith is unlike ordinary terrestrial soil.
   - Source: https://www.lpi.usra.edu/publications/books/lunar_sourcebook/pdf/Chapter09.pdf

2. `apollo_hfe_final_technical_report.pdf`
   - Instrument design, deployment, sensors, borestems, drill, calibration, and
     how the Apollo Heat Flow Experiment was meant to work.
   - Source: https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/Lunar/urn-nasa-pds-apollodoc/document_common/lunar_heat_flow_experiment_final_technical_report.pdf

3. `langseth1976_revised_lunar_heat_flow_values.pdf`
   - The canonical Apollo 15 and Apollo 17 heat-flow values used as basal-flux
     inputs in the model.
   - Source: https://www.lpi.usra.edu/meetings/lsc1976/pdf/1161.pdf

4. `hayne2017_global_regolith_thermophysical_properties_author_manuscript.pdf`
   - The conductivity/density model family used by the project, including the
     graded regolith and radiative thermal conductivity term.
   - Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1711.00977
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/2017JE005387

5. `vasavada2012_diviner_equatorial_temperatures.pdf`
   - Diviner surface-temperature modeling and the near-surface regolith
     thermophysical context behind Hayne-style models.
   - Source: https://luna1.diviner.ucla.edu/~dap/pubs/057.pdf
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1029/2011JE003987

6. `hemingway1973_specific_heats_lunar_soils_basalt_breccias.pdf`
   - Specific heat measurements used to justify the solver's heat-capacity
     treatment.
   - ADS: https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/1973LPSC....4.2481H/abstract

## Cross-checks and context

7. `grott2010_apollo_heat_flow_revisited_epsc_abstract.pdf`
   - Short summary of the Apollo thermal-conductivity reassessment.
   - Source: https://meetingorganizer.copernicus.org/EPSC2010/EPSC2010-49-1.pdf
   - Full-paper DOI: https://doi.org/10.1029/2010JE003612

8. `grott2010_apollo_thermal_conductivity_lpsc_abstract.pdf`
   - Companion LPSC abstract on the Apollo thermal-conductivity experiment.
   - Source: https://elib.dlr.de/63808/1/Grott_LPSC_2010.pdf

9. `martinez2021_model_lpsc2022_summary.pdf`
   - Open conference summary of the Martinez and Siegler low-temperature
     lunar-regolith conductivity model.
   - Source: https://www.hou.usra.edu/meetings/lpsc2022/pdf/2754.pdf
   - Full-paper DOI: https://doi.org/10.1029/2021JE006829
   - Code: https://zenodo.org/records/12586656

10. `feng2020_thermal_gradient_lpsc_abstract.pdf`
    - Open conference abstract related to CE-2 microwave + Diviner constraints
      on lunar regolith thermal gradients.
    - Source: https://www.hou.usra.edu/meetings/lpsc2020/pdf/2786.pdf
    - Full-paper DOI: https://doi.org/10.1029/2019JE006130

11. `white2022_apollo_hfe_implications_lpsc_abstract.pdf`
    - Recent Apollo HFE context and implications for lunar thermophysical
      properties and heat flow.
    - Source: https://www.hou.usra.edu/meetings/lpsc2022/pdf/2485.pdf

12. `chaste2025_chandrayaan3_insitu_thermal_conductivity.pdf`
    - Modern landed in-situ lunar thermal-conductivity comparison point.
    - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-91866-4

## Notes

- PDFs in this folder are for local study only.
- The most important missing full paper is Nagihara et al. 2018, because it is
  the restored Apollo temperature record that the project compares against.
- If you later drop manual PDFs into this folder, keep filenames short and use
  the citation key first, for example `nagihara2018_restored_apollo_hfe.pdf`.
